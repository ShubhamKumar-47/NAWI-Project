Controlled Change-Control Protocol (OIML R 76 §A.3)

Approved test records and certificates are legally protected against direct mutation. Initiating this controlled revision will:

  • Permanently archive the current reports and point-in-time data snapshots.
  • Mark current reports as superseded (historical versions are never overwritten).
  • Increment the revision counter to Revision #{{ session.revision_number|add:1 }}.
  • Unlock the observation tables so laboratory technicians can enter corrected or remedial measurements.
  • Require formal re-review and re-approval before new certificates can be issued.
